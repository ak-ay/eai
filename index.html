Questions before you apply.

Key answers for SUNY job-ready programs and TIU professional doctoral pathways.

Who can apply for the SUNY Job-Ready Skills Programs?

The programs are open to students and working professionals who have completed 12th grade or an undergraduate degree, subject to individual program eligibility criteria.

How are the SUNY courses delivered?

Courses are delivered online through the Skillsoft LMS, with study materials, recorded lectures, assignments, tutorials, and assessments.

Who issues the SUNY certificate?

Certificates are awarded directly by State University of New York, Potsdam and include a secure QR code for verification.

Are the programs accredited?

Yes. The programs are recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

What is a Professional Doctorate?

A Professional Doctorate is awarded based on a candidate's professional experience and requires a minimum of 15 years of work experience.

What credentials does The Thames International University hold?

TIU, based in Paris, is a private institution operating under France's legal framework (Code de l'Education, Articles L444-1 to R444-28). As an independent "hors contrat" university, it is authorized by the French Ministry of National Education to deliver online English-language programs, including EPDs, for global professionals.

How do employers view a Professional Doctorate?

Employers worldwide, with 65% in a 2023 QS Education report, see Professional Doctorates as equal to or more relevant than traditional PhDs for leadership roles. Professional Doctorates are increasingly valued by employers worldwide for their strong focus on leadership, innovation, and practical expertise. These qualifications demonstrate advanced strategic thinking and industry relevance, positioning professionals as capable leaders and change-makers in their respective fields.

How can an online EPD boost your career in India?

An online European Professional Doctorate (EPD) enhances your global academic profile and strengthens your professional credibility in today's competitive market. The qualification supports career advancement by positioning professionals for leadership, consulting, and specialized strategic roles across industries. A 2024 LinkedIn India report shows 45% higher earnings for international doctorate holders.
